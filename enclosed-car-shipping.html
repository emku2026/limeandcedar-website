Enclosed car shipping

For the cars that shouldn't see rain, road salt or a stone chip on the way: classics, exotics, luxury cars and fresh restorations — shipped in a fully covered trailer, nationwide.

When enclosed transport is worth it

  • Classic and collector cars
  • Exotic and luxury cars (Porsche, Ferrari, Lamborghini, high-end Mercedes and BMW)
  • New paint, wraps or ceramic coating
  • Low-clearance cars that need a liftgate or ramps
  • Cars going to shows, auctions or a buyer who'll inspect every inch

How we handle high-value cars

Enclosed transport FAQ

How much more does enclosed shipping cost?

Typically 40–80% more than open transport on the same route, because the trailer carries fewer cars. Send the form for an exact price.

Is my car insured for its full value?

We check that the carrier's cargo insurance covers your car's value before dispatch. Tell us the value when you request a quote.

Can you ship a non-running classic car?

Yes, with a carrier that has a winch. Please mention it in the form.
