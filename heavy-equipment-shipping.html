The right trailer for the load

TrailerBest for
FlatbedLower, lighter machines: skid steers, forklifts, small tractors
Step deckTaller loads that are too high for a flatbed: mid-size tractors, loaders
RGN / lowboyTall and heavy machines: excavators, dozers, big loaders — the front detaches so equipment drives on
HotshotSmall single machines on shorter routes, often faster

Oversize loads, permits and pilot cars

A load is oversize when it's wider than 8'6", taller than about 13'6" on the trailer, or the truck and load weigh more than 80,000 lb. Then every state on the route needs a permit, and wider loads need pilot cars. We plan the route and arrange the permits and escorts — they're included in your quote, not added later.

Bought at an auction?

After an equipment auction, transport is up to the buyer — and the yard wants the machine picked up on time. Send us the lot link and the pickup deadline; we'll find a carrier who can load at the yard and bring it to your site or farm.

What we need for a price

  • Pickup and delivery ZIP codes
  • Exact make and model (we can look up dimensions and weight), or L × W × H and weight
  • Attachments: bucket, blade, ripper — on or off?
  • Does it run, and how it will be loaded
  • Dates and any deadlines at pickup

Heavy equipment shipping FAQ

How much does it cost to ship heavy equipment?

It depends on the trailer the machine needs, distance, and whether the load is oversize. Legal-size loads on a flatbed or step deck cost less than RGN loads, and permits and pilot cars add to oversize moves. Send the form with the model and ZIP codes for a real price.

Do you handle permits and pilot cars?

Yes. If your load is oversize or overweight, we arrange state permits and pilot cars along the route and include them in your quote.

Can you pick up from an auction yard?

Yes. Send us the lot link and the yard's pickup deadline. We find a carrier who can load at the yard and deliver to your site.

Does the machine need to run?

No, but a non-running machine needs a winch, forklift or crane at pickup. Tell us in the form so we send the right truck.

Should I remove the bucket or attachments?

Sometimes removing a bucket or blade keeps a machine within legal size and saves the cost of permits. We'll tell you when it's worth it.
