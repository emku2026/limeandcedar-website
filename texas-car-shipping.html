Car shipping to and from Texas

Moving to Houston, selling a truck in Dallas, or bringing home an auction car from San Antonio? We ship cars, pickups, motorcycles and equipment door to door between Texas and every continental state.

Texas is one of the busiest states for car shipping

Texas has four of the largest metro areas in the country — Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, San Antonio and Austin — and steady traffic in both directions: people moving in from California and the Northeast, students heading to UT and Texas A&M, dealers buying at the big Texas auctions. More trucks on the road means faster pickup and better prices.

Carriers run Texas mainly on I-10 (California ↔ Houston ↔ Florida), I-20 and I-30 (Dallas ↔ the Southeast), I-35 (San Antonio ↔ Austin ↔ Dallas ↔ the Midwest) and I-40 through Amarillo. Pickups in the big metros are the fastest; rural West Texas and the Panhandle can take a few extra days because fewer trucks pass through.

Weather and seasons

  • Hurricane season (June–November) can close roads around Houston and the Gulf Coast for a few days. If a storm is forecast, carriers wait it out — your car stays safe, delivery moves.
  • Winter ice in North Texas is rare but shuts down I-35 and I-20 when it happens. A one- or two-day delay is normal.
  • Summer heat: nothing to worry about for the car, but keep about ¼ tank of fuel and make sure the battery is healthy if the car will sit at a terminal or auction yard.

Auction cars and equipment from Texas

Texas has some of the largest vehicle auction yards in the country — Copart and IAA locations around Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio and Austin, plus dealer auctions. Send us the lot number and the yard address; if the car doesn't run, we book a carrier with a winch. Auction yards charge storage after a few free days, so tell us the pickup deadline.

We also haul tractors, excavators and oilfield equipment in and out of Texas on flatbed, step deck and RGN trailers, with permits for oversize loads.

Just moved to Texas?

Texas gives new residents 30 days to register an out-of-state vehicle. Requirements change, so check the current steps on the Texas DMV "New to Texas" page before your car arrives.

Texas car shipping FAQ

How long does it take to ship a car from California to Texas?

Usually 3–4 days on the road, plus 1–5 days to get your car picked up after booking. Los Angeles to Houston is about 1,550 miles; the Bay Area to Dallas is about 1,730.

Do you ship within Texas, for example Houston to Dallas?

Yes. In-state moves between the big Texas cities are common and usually take 1 day once the car is picked up.

Can you pick up a car from a Copart or IAA yard in Texas?

Yes. Send us the lot number, yard address and any pickup deadline. Non-running cars are loaded by carriers with a winch.

Do I pay anything before a carrier is assigned?

No. You get the deposit invoice only after a carrier is assigned to your vehicle. The balance is paid to the driver at delivery.
