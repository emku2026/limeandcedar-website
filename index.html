What affects the price

  • Distance & route — busy interstate routes are cheaper per mile.
  • Vehicle size — a pickup takes more space than a sedan.
  • Open or enclosed — enclosed costs more.
  • Runs or not — inoperable vehicles need a winch.
  • Season — snowbird months and summer are busier.

Typical open-trailer prices: regional $300–$700, half-country $700–$1,100, coast to coast $1,000–$1,600.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to ship a car?

It depends on distance, route, vehicle size, open or enclosed trailer and the season. Short regional moves often start around $300; coast-to-coast moves are usually $1,000–$1,600 on an open trailer. Send the form and we will give you a real price, not a teaser.

When do I pay?

Nothing when you book. The deposit is invoiced only after a carrier is assigned to your vehicle. The rest is paid to the driver on delivery.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier must have active FMCSA authority and cargo insurance. Your vehicle is inspected and photographed at pickup and at delivery.

How long does shipping take?

Pickup is usually 1–5 days after booking. Transit is about 1–3 days for short routes and 7–10 days coast to coast.

Do you ship motorcycles?

Yes. Motorcycles ship strapped on a trailer or in an enclosed truck, door to door, anywhere in the continental U.S.

Are you a broker or a carrier?

We are a licensed auto transport broker (MC-1543888). We match your vehicle with a vetted carrier on your route and stay your single point of contact from booking to delivery.
