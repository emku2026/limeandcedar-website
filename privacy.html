Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Lime and Cedar LLC ("we", "us"), an auto transport broker (MC-1543888). Contact: limencedar@gmail.com, (909) 323-0321.

What we collect

  • Information you enter in the quote form: pickup and delivery locations, vehicle, dates, the price you suggest, your name, phone number and email.
  • Technical data sent by your browser, such as IP address, used to protect the form from abuse.
  • If you book: the details in your booking confirmation and your electronic signature.

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote and contact you about your shipment by phone, text message or email.
  • To arrange transport: we share the pickup and delivery details, vehicle information and contact person with the carrier assigned to your vehicle.
  • To send invoices through our payment processor (PayPal).
  • To keep records required by law.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information and do not share it with other brokers or marketers.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about your quote and shipment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing limencedar@gmail.com, unless we must keep it for an active shipment or by law. California residents may request to know what personal information we hold about them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
