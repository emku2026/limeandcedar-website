About Lime & Cedar Auto Transport

A small, licensed auto transport broker from the San Francisco Bay Area, shipping cars and motorcycles across the continental U.S.

Who we are

Lime & Cedar Auto Transport is operated by Lime and Cedar LLC, registered with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration as a property broker: MC-1543888, USDOT 4056326. You can check our license on the FMCSA website.

What a broker does for you

We don't own trucks. We work with thousands of licensed carriers and find the right one for your route and dates, check that its authority and insurance are active, and handle the paperwork, payments and updates. You get one contact person from the first call to delivery.

How we work

  • Honest prices. We quote what carriers actually accept, so your car gets picked up on time.
  • No deposit until a carrier is assigned.
  • Everything online: e-signed booking confirmation, invoices you can pay by card, PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay or bank, and a signed PDF copy for your records.
  • A real person. Call or text (909) 323-0321.
