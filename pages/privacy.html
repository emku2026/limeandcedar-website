What we collect

What we don't do

We do not sell your information and do not share it with other brokers or marketers.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about your quote and shipment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit the site, which pages they view and how they found us. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives technical data such as your browser type, approximate location and the pages you visit. It does not receive what you type in the quote form. You can block it with your browser's cookie settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

ZIP code lookup

When you type a ZIP code in the quote form, the page sends only that ZIP code to the free service zippopotam.us to show the matching city, so you can see the ZIP is right. No other information is sent to it.
