We're based in the San Francisco Bay Area, so California is home. Moving out of LA, bringing a car to San Diego, or shipping a classic from Sacramento — we move cars, pickups, motorcycles and equipment door to door between California and every continental state.

California is the biggest car shipping market in the country

More cars ship in and out of California than any other state. The two big hubs are Southern California (Los Angeles, Orange County, the Inland Empire, San Diego) and Northern California (the Bay Area, San Jose, Sacramento). Pickups in these metros are the fastest and the cheapest. The Central Valley, the North Coast and mountain towns can take a few extra days because fewer trucks pass through.

Carriers leave California on four main roads: I-5 north to Oregon and Washington, I-80 east from the Bay Area over Donner Pass toward Reno, Salt Lake City and Chicago, I-15 from Southern California to Las Vegas and Utah, and I-10 and I-40 east toward Arizona, Texas and the South.

Moving out of California?

Many of our customers are leaving California for Texas, Arizona, Nevada, Idaho, Tennessee and Florida. Shipping the car lets you fly and skip a multi-day drive. Trucks leaving California fill quickly in summer, so book 1–2 weeks ahead if you can.

Moving to California?

California asks new residents to register an out-of-state vehicle within 20 days. Most cars also need a California smog check and a VIN verification. Requirements change, so check the current steps on the California DMV website before your car arrives.

Weather and seasons

  • Winter in the mountains (December–March): snow on I-80 over Donner Pass and on I-5 through the Siskiyous and the Grapevine can close the road or require chains. A one- or two-day delay is normal.
  • Wildfire season (summer and fall): fires sometimes close highways for a day or two. Carriers reroute, and we keep you posted.
  • Snowbird season: in fall many cars head to Southern California and Arizona, and back north in spring. Book early in October–January.

Auction cars, motorcycles and equipment

California has many Copart and IAA auction yards around Los Angeles, the Inland Empire, San Diego, the Bay Area, Sacramento and Fresno. Send us the lot number and the yard address; if the car doesn't run, we book a carrier with a winch. Yards charge storage after a few free days, so tell us the pickup deadline.

We also ship motorcycles, classics and luxury cars in enclosed trailers, and tractors, excavators and farm equipment from the Central Valley and anywhere else in the state.

How it works

California car shipping FAQ

How long does it take to ship a car from California to the East Coast?

Usually 5–6 days on the road, plus 1–5 days to get your car picked up after booking. Los Angeles to Miami is about 2,730 miles; San Francisco to New York is about 2,900.

Do you ship within California, for example Los Angeles to San Francisco?

Yes. Los Angeles to the Bay Area is about 380 miles and usually takes 1 day once the car is picked up.

Can you pick up a car from a Copart or IAA yard in California?

Yes. Send us the lot number, yard address and any pickup deadline. Non-running cars are loaded by carriers with a winch.

Do I pay anything before a carrier is assigned?

No. You get the deposit invoice only after a carrier is assigned to your vehicle. The balance is paid to the driver at delivery.
